# Mielina support

Mielina helps you keep track of your symptoms and your medication. It doesn't give medical advice and doesn't replace your neurologist.

## Report a problem

In the app, open Settings › About Mielina › Report a problem and tap Write the email. Your email app opens with an email to the developer that already holds the app's version, the phone's system and the errors the app noted, with room at the top for what happened. You can read and change all of it, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

If no email app opens, the app offers to copy the report, so you can paste it into an email to mielina.support@gmail.com. You can also write to that address yourself: say which phone you use and, if you can, which version of Mielina, as Report a problem shows it.

## Questions

Questions about the app or about privacy can be sent to [mielina.support@gmail.com](mailto:mielina.support@gmail.com). Mielina is made by Alex Raducanu as a personal project, not by a company. Questions about your health are for your neurologist or your doctor.

## A new phone

Everything you log stays on your phone, and Mielina has no account to sign in to. To take your records to a new phone, make a backup with Settings › Backup › Back up and save or share the file where the new phone can reach it, then open it there with Settings › Backup › Restore from a backup. A restore replaces everything the app holds on that phone, settings included.

## Privacy

The [privacy policy](privacy.html) says what stays on your phone, what leaves it and why.
